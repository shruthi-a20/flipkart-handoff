HANDOFF - interactive prototype (Flipkart APM 2029, Prompt 2)

HOW TO RUN
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
   No installation, no server and no internet needed. Built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript only.

HOW TO USE
- Tap the highlighted button on each screen (a "Tap here" hand appears after a moment).
- Press the play button (bottom left) to autoplay the whole story (about 2 minutes).
- Use the left and right arrow keys to step back and forward.
- Best viewed full screen (F11).

The story follows one silent water pump in Ward 34, Chennai, the day after the Snap,
across 21 steps and 9 users. All names, places and numbers are fictional demo data.
